### Reflection
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;From a talk entitled "Project Management and Project Development" delivered by Serunai Commerce Sdn Bhd, I learnt several important things including time management, highlighted skillsets and project management phases. <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Initially, the speaker highlighted that planning skills are among the important skills to own as lacking it causes one to fail. In addition, I learnt that project management is somethings that relates to us very closely in our daily life.
It consists of defining, designing, developing, testing and deployment. An analogy of preparing Nasi Lemak was provided to foster a stronger understanding on this matter. Overall, the talk piqued a realization in me that skills applied in our daily life can be connected to project developing.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As an improvement for the talk, an online quiz platform like Quizizz and Question and Answers platform like Slido can be provided to engage audiences with the talk.
