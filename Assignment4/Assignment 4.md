### Reflection
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The visit to Johor Port Berhad had opened an insight on how ICT works within a large organization. ICT enables automation, which significantly reduces workload and improves operational efficiency. As nearby ports continue to develop rapidly, Johor Port must remain competitive by adopting digitalization within their organization. This experience increased my self-awareness regarding the importance of technology evolution as well as motivated me to keep improving my skills. <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As a suggestion for improvement, future visits could include sessions with employees from different organizational levels, rather than focusing only on administrative staff, to gain more diverse perspectives and insights.
