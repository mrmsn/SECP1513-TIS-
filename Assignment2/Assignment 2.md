### Reflection

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Attending the talk given by PPG Industry, I learnt that industries nowadays rely heavily on efficient communication and structured planning and organizational processes. <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;These can be seen in their explanation of their CoEs namely Data Analytics, Global Infrastructure and SAP. First, data-driven decisions are made based on the data they collect. After that, based on the data, they plan, build and run their projects relying on predictions and business demands. 
They also utilize Systems, Applications and Products in Data Processing (SAP) as a modular enterprise solution for various business needs and challenges.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In addition, they highlight the comparison between university-scale projects and industry-scale projects, the future of IT infrastructure and their customer services related to product creation. This helped me begin honing both my technical skills as well as my soft skills.
