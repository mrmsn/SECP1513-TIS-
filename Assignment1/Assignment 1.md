### Reflection
<p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The visit at UTM Digital helped me to understand that IT and System Development do not only rely on the technology advancement, but also on planning, problem-solving and communication skills among teammates. It gave us a clearer vision on the purposes of the assignments and how they relate to real working experiences. From the talk, i got to learn about waterfall and scrum method which opens a new insight for me on how to complete assignments in groups. In my opinion, completing a work or projects using scrum method would bring the best outcome as we are able to get a grasp on the possible final result as well as getting reviews before completing them. Besides that, we are also able to find out on how different technologies work and collaborate in various ways for a single system to function effectively. This can be found when we were interviewing on the "Archibus" booth. Overall, this visit helped us to gain valuable skills to be applied and understand how our study relates to our future careers. <br> 
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As an improvement, online quizzes on platform like quizizz or longer interaction sessions could be provided so participants can gain deeper understanding and practical exposure.</p>
